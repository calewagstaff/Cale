# Cale

## Will's Spelling Stadium (`spelling-stadium/index.html`)

A football- and basketball-themed spelling practice app for a fifth grader.

- **Coach's Corner** (for parents): paste the week's words, one per line. Add an optional clue sentence after a colon, e.g. `receive: Will will receive the kickoff.` Saved lists can be switched, edited, or deleted.
- **Warm-Ups**: look, cover, spell, check.
- **Scramble Drill**: put the fumbled letter tiles back in order.
- **Touchdown Drive**: each correct word moves the ball downfield; a miss means retyping the word correctly, and it comes back later in the drive.
- **Buzzer Beater**: a 60-second shootout. 2 points a word, 3 after a streak of three.
- A stat sheet tracks each word's rights and misses, flags "tough words" for extra practice, and marks words mastered.

Words are read aloud so they stay hidden while Will spells. When published as a claude.ai artifact, lists and scores are saved to the artifact. Opened as a plain local file, they're saved in the browser.

## Pink Plates (`pink-plates/index.html`)

A pink and lilac gym diary for a teen lifter.

- **Log**: date, minutes, focus (legs & glutes, upper body, full body, core & pilates, cardio, other), a mood check, and exercises with sets of reps × lbs. Quick-add chips cover common lifts, and adding an exercise you've done before fills in your last sets.
- **History**: every past workout, newest first and grouped by month, with a 16-week calendar, search, and focus filters. Tap a workout to see its sets and notes, then do it again, edit it, or delete it.
- **PRs**: the heaviest weight logged for each exercise. New PRs get called out when you save.
- A weekly goal (hearts), a week streak, and total pounds lifted sit at the top.

When published as a claude.ai artifact, workouts are saved to the artifact. Opened as a plain local file, they're saved in the browser.
